\chapter{Functional Strumming Patterns / 功能性扫弦模式}
\label{ch:strumming}

\section{Overview / 概述}

\eng{Strumming patterns are the foundation of rhythm guitar in math rock. Unlike traditional rock or pop, math rock often employs odd time signatures and syncopated rhythms.}

\chn{扫弦节奏是数学摇滚节奏吉他的基础。与传统摇滚或流行不同，数学摇滚常用奇数拍号和切分节奏。}

\enandcn
  {Let's start with common time signatures.}
  {从最常用的拍号开始。}

\paragraph{核心术语}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \term{奇数拍号}{Odd Time Signature}
  \item \term{切分音}{Syncopation}
  \item \term{连音符}{Tuplet}
\end{itemize}

\begin{notebox}[提示]
  数学摇滚扫弦常强调反拍。练习时先用节拍器慢速感受重音移位。
\end{notebox}

\section{3/4 Time / 四三拍}

\begin{examplebox}[3/4 扫弦模式]
  3/4 拍中常见的扫弦模式：
\end{examplebox}

\begin{verse}
  \small\ttfamily
  e|-----------------|-----------------|\\
  B|-----------------|-----------------|\\

  D|-------2---------|-------2---------|\\
  A|-----3---3-------|-----3---3-------|\\
  E|---3-------3-----|---3-------3-----|\\

\eng{This pattern uses palm muting on the downbeats with open strings ringing.}
\chn{这一模式使用下拍制音，空弦音延续。}

\section{4/4 Time / 四四拍}

\eng{In 4/4, math rock strumming creates tension by emphasizing the \& of 2 and \& of 4.}
\chn{在 4/4 拍中，数学摇滚扫弦通过强调第 2、4 拍反拍制造张力。}

\paragraph{Functional Strumming Patterns in 4/4}
\eng{You might have heard strumming pattern 5A or something similar at the beginning of or in the breakdown section of math rock/Midwest emo songs. It is a common pattern but usually has slight variations. In example 5B, the second strum is longer and the last two notes should be played as dead notes (muted strings).}

\chn{你可能在数学摇滚/中西部情绪摇滚歌曲的开头或过门部分听过 5A 或类似的扫弦模式。这是一个常见的模式，但通常会有细微变化。在示例 5B 中，第二次扫弦更长，最后两个音应作为死音（哑音）的弹奏。}

\eng{Strumming pattern 5C has a short staccato first strum followed by regular eighth-note strums. Adding dead notes before each phrase gives an interesting feel, as shown in 5D. Example 5E often appears with palm muting as a section builds toward something.}

\chn{5C 模式以简短的断音第一扫开始，随后是常规四分之一音符的扫弦。在每个乐句前加死音会产生有趣的感觉，如 5D 所示。示例 5E 通常出现在用掌制音时，作为一段音乐向高潮推进。}

\eng{Strumming pattern 5F is great for math-pop ideas. It has a poppy groove with syncopation that makes it less conventional than typical pop. You can also play it without the rests for a slightly different feel.}

\chn{5F 模式非常适合数学流行风格。它具有流行感的律动，加上切分，使其相比普通流行更不寻常。你也可以不加休止符地演奏它，感觉会略有不同。}

\eng{Strumming pattern 5H fills in all the gaps while retaining characteristic syncopation. It works well for an upbeat intro. Pattern 5I has a lot of energy and can be used to great effect in verses or bridges, especially if you palm-mute some intervals. Example 5J replaces some strummed notes with dead notes for an even more upbeat feel.}

\chn{5H 模式填满了所有空隙，同时保留了典型的切分感。它很适合作为欢快的开头。5I 具有很强的能量，可在主歌或桥段中大放异彩，尤其是当你对某些音进行掌制音时。示例 5J 用死音替换了一些扫弦音，听起来更有活力。}

\paragraph{Two-Guitar Strumming Patterns}
\eng{Let's move on to functional strumming patterns for two guitars. Example 5K is based around a pattern used by Meet Me In St. Louis. The alternating pattern is the same for the first two beats, then it varies from beat three onwards. Panning the parts left and right with this kind of idea sounds excellent.}

\chn{接下来是适合两把吉他的功能性扫弦模式。示例 5K 基于 Meet Me In St. Louis 使用的一个模式。前两拍的交替模式相同，从第三拍开始则会变化。这样的想法在左右声道分开时听起来非常棒。}

\eng{You could play the pattern on one guitar, but you will miss the separation effect of two guitars. Example 5L shows a groove where one of the beats is syncopated, typically the last beat of the bar. The first bar uses a unified guitar pattern and the second bar alternates between two guitars.}

\chn{你可以在一把吉他上演奏这个模式，但你会错过两把吉他分离的效果。示例 5L 展示了一个律动，其中一个拍通常是最后一个拍的切分。第一小节使用统一的吉他模式，第二小节则在两把吉他之间交替。}

\eng{Finally, pattern 5M is excellent for creating space in a song while maintaining an upbeat feel with staccato eighth notes in one guitar. You can experiment with the placement of the full-chord guitar, or even play the second guitar part as arpeggios for contrast. Since the chord is only strummed once per bar, it is a good place to add reverb, delay, or modulation to fill out the sound.}

\chn{最后，5M 模式非常适合在歌曲中创造空间，同时一把吉他的断音八分音符仍然保持欢快感。你可以尝试调整全和弦吉他的放置位置，甚至可以让第二把吉他演奏琶音以形成对比。由于每小节只扫弦一次，这是一个适合添加混响、延迟或调制以丰富音色的好机会。}

\section{3/4 Time / 四三拍}

\eng{Moving on to 3/4 time. This is one of my favourite time signatures (along with 7/8). They are a great way to inject energy into a song because the bar changes usually feel quicker due to the shorter length.} 

\chn{接下来是 3/4 拍。这是我最喜欢的拍号之一（还有 7/8）。它们是给歌曲注入能量的好方法，因为小节更短，听起来变化更快。}

\eng{The following are some common 3/4 strumming pattern variations you will hear in math rock bands. Example 6A accents beat three. This idea is taken from the song “Pretend” by Via Luna. If you really accent the final strum, it is a lot of fun.}

\chn{下面是一些在数学摇滚乐队中常见的 3/4 扫弦模式变体。示例 6A 强调第三拍。这个想法来自 Via Luna 的“Pretend”。如果你真正强调最后一扫，会非常有趣。}

\eng{Strumming pattern 6B takes the same idea but arpeggiates the chord in the same pattern, keeping the accent on beat three. Pattern 6C is inspired by Totorro's “Home Alone” and is a fantastic driving pattern that works well in most songs.}

\chn{6B 模式采用相同的想法，但在相同模式中拨奏和弦，仍然保持第三拍的重音。6C 模式则受到 Totorro 的“Home Alone”启发，是一个出色的推进型模式，适用于大多数歌曲。}

\eng{Pattern 6D uses the same idea and shows how it can be adapted to 5/8 time for an interesting variation. You can count the bar as 1 \& 2 \& 3 or as 5 eighth notes to keep the feel of 3/4 with an abrupt ending. Pattern 6E is inspired by Blakfish's “Preparing for Guests” and injects energy into a song. Pattern 6F takes the same idea and completes the phrase with an arpeggio.}

\chn{6D 模式采用相同的思路，并展示了如何将其改编到 5/8 拍以获得有趣的变化。你可以把小节计为 1 \& 2 \& 3，或者作为 5 个八分音符，以保留 3/4 的感觉同时有一种突兀的结束。6E 模式受到 Blakfish 的“Preparing for Guests”启发，为歌曲注入能量。6F 模式则用琶音完整地结束乐句。}

\section{5/8 Time / 五八拍}

\eng{Now, let's delve into an unusual time signature: 5/8 time. I included this because it is a logical progression from 3/4 time. In 3/4, we count three quarter notes. In 5/8, there are five eighth notes. If we subdivide 3/4 into eighth notes, it becomes six eighth notes. Thus 5/8 can feel like a shortened bar of 3/4.} 

\chn{现在，让我们深入探讨不寻常的拍号：5/8 拍。我之所以包含它，是因为它是从 3/4 拍的一个合理延伸。在 3/4 拍中，我们数三个四分音符。在 5/8 拍中，则是五个八分音符。如果把 3/4 拆分成八分音符，就变成了六个八分音符。因此 5/8 会感觉像是一个缩短的 3/4 小节。}

\eng{You can think of 5/8 as 1 \& 2 \& 3, with the absence of the \& of 3 giving the bar a cut-short feel. The example videos will help if the rhythm is hard to work out. In 5/8, sixteenth notes are noted with + symbols for clarity.} 

\chn{你可以把 5/8 视为 1 \& 2 \& 3，缺少第三拍的 \& 会让小节感觉被截短。如果节奏难以掌握，可以看示例视频。为了清楚起见，5/8 中的十六分音符用 + 符号表示。}

\eng{It is common to accent the 1st and either the 3rd or 4th beats in 5/8. Pattern 7A accents 1 and 3. Pattern 7B applies palm muting between those accents. Pattern 7C accents 1 and 4, and 7D adds another accent on beat 5. Pattern 7E combines palm muting with 1 and 4 accents, while 7F introduces more space and is an excellent way to make 5/8 feel breathable.}

\chn{在 5/8 中，通常会强调第一拍和第三拍或第四拍。7A 模式强调一和三。7B 模式在这些重音之间加入掌制音。7C 模式强调一和四，而 7D 在第五拍上再加一个重音。7E 结合了掌制音和一、四拍重音，而 7F 则引入更多空间，是让 5/8 听起来更通透的绝佳方式。}
